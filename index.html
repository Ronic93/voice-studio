✦ Voice Studio

Character voices for your Seedance clips. Runs fully on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
Voice cloning (copy a voice from a clip) is too heavy for a phone. Send a short clip of your own voice, or of someone who gave consent, to Grok Bot in chat and it'll make the line for you.

The first load downloads the voice model (~90 MB) once. After that it's cached. Engine: Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0).
